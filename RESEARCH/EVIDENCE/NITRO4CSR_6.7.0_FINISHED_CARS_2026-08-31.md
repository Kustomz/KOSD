# Nitro4CSR — 6.7.0 Finished-Car Status Evidence

**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Source:** Screenshot supplied by Kustomz

Nitro4CSR stated that finished 6.7.0 cars had been added to his database, while RX-7, MX-5, and Lotus Emira i4 were being held back because he suspected changes to those cars.

## KOSD Research Interpretation

This is evidence that a third-party car database can intentionally distinguish **finished** cars from known cars that are **held back pending suspected changes**.

It must therefore not be used as a binary test for whether a car exists in CSR2 game data or whether its Unity assets are present.

This evidence supports maintaining separate states/signals for:

- game-data identity/reference;
- Unity asset presence;
- asset completeness/finished state;
- release/live usability;
- third-party database publication status.

This document is research evidence only and does not establish a finalized KOS rule.
