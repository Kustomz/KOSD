# KOS-CORE-001

**Version:** 2.0.0 (Draft)

## Purpose

Defines the core operating principles of the Kustomz Operating System.

## Principles

1. Files are the authoritative source of truth.
2. Conversations are temporary workspaces.
3. Updates improve the KOS; they do not replace it.
4. Locked assets are authoritative until explicitly changed, superseded, or unlocked by an explicit Kustomz owner decision under the adopted KOS Decision & Promotion Protocol.
5. Stable IDs are permanent.
